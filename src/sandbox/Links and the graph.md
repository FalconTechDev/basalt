# Links and the graph

A link is two square brackets around a note name: [[Start here]]. The other note then lists this one under **backlinks**.

Tags such as #graph group notes without a folder.

## The graph

Open the graph from the left ribbon. It draws every note as a dot and every link as a line.

- **2D** and **3D** show notes and their links.
- **Files** shows folders and files of this vault.
- **Code** shows functions and the calls between them in a code folder.

Right click a dot to hide it, focus on its neighbours, pin it or give it a colour.

See also [[Canvas]] and [[Code and the terminal]].
